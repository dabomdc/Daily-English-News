Mimi the Cute Cat!
Mimi is a small cat.
She has soft, white fur.
Mimi loves to play with a ball.
She drinks milk and takes long naps.
문장 버튼을 누르면 1문장씩 읽어줍니다.
WORDS (5)
cat고양이 · A furry pet animal.
soft부드러운 · Nice to touch, not hard.
play놀다 · To have fun with games.
milk우유 · A white drink, good for cats.
nap낮잠 · A short sleep, often during the day.
